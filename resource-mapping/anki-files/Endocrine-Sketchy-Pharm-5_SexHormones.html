   While letrozole is first-line, clomiphene is an alternative option that can be used to treat infertility due to anovulation (e.g., PCOS)

39.1 What medical therapy may be used for short-term treatment of symptomatic fibroids refractory to OCPs, progestin-IUDs, and NSAIDs?

[2]
   What medical therapy may be used for short-term treatment of symptomatic fibroids refractory to OCPs, progestin-IUDs, and NSAIDs?

GnRH agonists (e.g., leuprolide)
GnRH antagonists (e.g., elagolix)
- GnRH agonists desensitize the pituitary via overstimulation → diminished release of LH and FSH → reduced estrogen synthesis → volume reduction (deprives fibroid of its growth stimulus), amenorrhea, improved anemia

- They are an optimal treatment prior to surgery, but are not suited for long-term monotherapy (> 6 months) because they cause rebound growth and bone demineralization (osteoporosis). Fibroids continue to grow once therapy is discontinued!

- UpToDate authors prefer antagonists due to their milder side effects
