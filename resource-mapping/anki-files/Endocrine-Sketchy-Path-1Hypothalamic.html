- Both medication side effects and pituitary adenoma may cause elevated prolactin

- Presence of hypopituitarism (e.g., hypothyroidism, hypogonadism) helps distinguish pituitary adenoma from medication side effect due to compression of thyrotrophs and gonadotrophs in the ant. pituitary
