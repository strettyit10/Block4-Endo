What are the "4 P's" used to treat thyroid storm?

1. Propranolol
2. Propylthiouracil (PTU)
3. Prednisolone
4. Potassium iodide

Propranolol - blocks sympathetic effects and peripheral conversion of T4 to T3 (inhibits 5' deiodinase)

PTU - blocks thyroid hormone synthesis and ↓ peripheral conversion of T4 to T3

Prednisolone - inhibits peripheral conversion of T4 to T3 (inhibits 5' deiodinase) and prevents exophthalmopathy

Potassium iodide - inhibits proteolytic cleavage of T3 and T4 from thyroglobulin → inhibits thyroid hormone release (Wolff-Chaikoff effect)
