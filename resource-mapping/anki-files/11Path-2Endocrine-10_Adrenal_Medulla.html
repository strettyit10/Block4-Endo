2.1 Pheochromocytoma and neuroblastoma are diagnosed by increased serum and urine levels of catecholamines, or their breakdown products [3]
   Pheochromocytoma and neuroblastoma are diagnosed by increased serum and urine levels of catecholamines, or their breakdown products metanephrines, VMA, and HVA
- Metanephrines = metanephrine (epinephrine metabolite) and normetanephrine (norepinephrine metabolite)
- VMA = vanillylmandelic acid (breakdown product of metanephrines)
- HVA = homovanillic acid (breakdown product of dopamine)

- This card used to only say "pheochromocytoma" but neuroblastoma was added for completeness, additional clarification was added that HVA is NOT a metanephrine metabolite. The duplicate card (nid:1462818499640) has been marked for deletion (Oct 2025)

5.1
Treatment for a pheochromocytoma is [...]

  
Treatment for a pheochromocytoma is surgical excision*

*Before surgery, phenoxybenzamine (α-blocker) must be given first before β-blockers to prevent unopposed α1 stimulation and thereby hypertensive crisis

8.1
Pheochromocytoma is associated with the rule of [...]

  
Pheochromocytoma is associated with the rule of 10s

Rule of 10s:
10% malignant
10% bilateral
10% extra-adrenal (e.g., bladder wall, organ of Zuckerkandl)
10% calcify
10% children

9.1
Prior to removal of a pheochromocytoma, irreversible [...], such as phenoxybenzamine are administered, followed by [...]

  
Prior to removal of a pheochromocytoma, irreversible α-blockers, such as phenoxybenzamine are administered, followed by β-blockers

- α-blockade must be achieved before giving β-blockers to avoid hypertensive crisis

- May use phentolamine (a shorter acting α-blocker) intraoperatively if needed

The five episodic hyperadrenergic symptoms of a(n) pheochromocytoma are:

1. Pressure (increased BP)
2. Pain (headache)
3. Perspiration
4. Palpitations (tachycardia)
5. Pallor

In contrast with aldosteronoma → hypertension, hypokalemia (leading to fatigue, muscles weakness, headaches, and palpitations), metabolic alkalosis, and mild hypernatremia
