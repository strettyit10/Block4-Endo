What drug may be used to replace mineralocorticoids in primary adrenal insufficiency?

Fludrocortisone (aldosterone analog)

Fludrocortisone has minimal to no glucocorticoid side effects, so it can be combined with corticosteroids in replacement therapy without worrying about Cushing syndrome

4.1 Treatment of adrenal insufficiency:

- [...] for both primary and secondary
- Add fludrocortisone for [primary/secondary]

   Treatment of adrenal insufficiency:

- Glucocorticoids for both primary and secondary
- Add fludrocortisone for primary (Addison disease)

- Fludrocortisone replaces the aldosterone that the adrenal gland can no longer make

- Fludrocortisone is not necessary in secondary adrenal insufficiency since mineralocorticoids are still made (via the intact RAAS)
