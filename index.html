Recommended Study Workflow

A simple path from first-pass learning to board-style application.

1

Start with Study Guides

Build a structured overview of the lecture material and identify the highest-yield concepts first.

2

Use Active Recall Drills

Practice identifying vignettes, trap patterns, mechanisms, and key facts through active learning and repetition.

3

Reinforce with Databases

Return to organized references, tables, and quick-hit repositories when you need clarification or comparison.

4

Apply with Board-Style Practice

Shift into question-based reasoning and exam-style thinking using practice sets and external QBanks.

5

Track Progress

Log what is complete, what still feels weak, and where to focus your next review block.
